About the Website

Woodbridge Heating & Cooling

An enquiry website for property owners in Woodbridge.

Purpose

Start With the Property and the Work

Use this website to describe the property, equipment, and work you want reviewed. The service pages explain the available work and help you prepare an enquiry.

Coverage, contractor availability, qualifications for the proposed work, pricing, and terms should be established as part of the request and proposal process.

How Your Request Is Reviewed

The enquiry team receives your request and shares it with a relevant service provider for review. The team uses your contact details to ask about the property and proposed work.

The request starts a discussion. Before work is agreed, ask the provider to confirm coverage, the qualifications required for the task, the assessment scope, pricing and appointment availability. A form submission does not book a visit or request an emergency response.

The equipment guidance helps you prepare questions; it is not a diagnosis or a property inspection. Each service page links to relevant public technical guidance. Check the instructions for your actual equipment with a qualified contractor.

What to include

  • The equipment or system involved
  • Symptoms and when they started
  • Property city and any access constraints
  • The result you want from the work

Also include the property address, your contact details, and who can approve the work.
